# Riverside playtest

Open the game and choose **Explore the riverside** on the title screen.
The preview uses Sura and Kaya and does not write campaign saves. **Leave preview**
restores the previous session. If you leave the area, **Pause → Leave preview**
works too. Returning players can reach the area through Pella near the east path
in Ba Dan village.

1. Tap clear ground to walk. Drag to look around; pinch or scroll to zoom.
   **Follow party** brings the camera back.
2. Walk near Elder Mira and watch her greeting; tap her to talk. Try **Wave**.
   Use **Under the banyan**, then tap the open square: branches should cover
   your character and then reveal them as they step into sunlight.
3. Try **Water form** and **Fire form**. Watch the feet settle, the hands gather,
   the release and the recovery. Settings → Reduce motion must be off to see motion.
4. **Meet Pebble**, take a **Tea break**, then **Visit the shrine** and continue
   through its dialogue. Look for three discoveries and lights around the shrine.
5. Try **Dorin's drill** across the bridge. Complete water, fire, water in order.
   This is an animation practice sequence, with no combat damage or XP.
6. Drag back through the area and check whether the river, foliage and animal
   make you want to linger. Leave the preview when finished.

**Try a battle** opens the existing forest-road tactical encounter with the
preview party. **Pause → Return to the riverside** gets you back at any point.
This encounter still uses the campaign's current combat art and animation.

A screenshot of a spot where a character crosses a painted obstacle is useful.
Include your device, browser and orientation if taps or animation behave oddly.

The two preview heroes now use drawn walk, wave and bending poses. NPCs and
Pebble still use procedural art. More directions, animation cleanup, new combat
animation and a connected large overworld remain further work.
