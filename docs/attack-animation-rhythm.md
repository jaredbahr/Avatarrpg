# Element attack rhythm

Ranged character gestures now have distinct presentation timing: fire releases sharply, water gathers and extends smoothly, earth takes longer to set its weight with less forward travel, and air moves with a light flowing extension. Melee strikes, self-casts and unknown effect families retain their existing timing.

All styles use the existing wind-up, release and recovery drawings. Impact, sound, the extended hold and recovery continue to derive from the same timeline. Reduced motion still shortens the entire sequence and suppresses particles and camera shake. No combat outcomes or asset formats change.

To compare, use fire, water, earth and air ranged abilities during combat. Watch the preparation, release and return to guard; for long throws the actor should hold the extension until impact. Repeat with Reduce motion enabled. Canvas and WebGL consume the same tracks.
