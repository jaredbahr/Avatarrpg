# Saltstep: what is on the market map, who lives where, and what the places are

**Status: DRAFT for Jared (the writing pass, 2026-11-19).** These are the story's answers to issue #895 and to the
placeholders in the market brief (`docs/places/saltstep_market.md`, blockout v2, 32 by 18). They are facts for the
level design to build on, not lines. Where a fact was already in the game, the bible or a script it is marked
**(shipped)**, **(bible)** or **(script)**; the rest is new and is listed at the end for the owner. Acts III to V
are plan-grade scripts, so their facts may still move.

## The three map answers

### 1. The courier office: on this map, as a door only

- **The party never walks to it.** Scene 2.1 only says the office "keeps the other halves". In Scene 2.3 the
  customs clerk "sends a boy along the pole walk to the courier office. The boy comes back with the other half."
  **(shipped: `mq02_stall_receipt`, loss branch only)**. The objective at that point is the customs stall, not the
  office. The issue's "Scene 2.1 sends the party there" is not what is built.
- **So it is a front, not a room:** a door, a letter board and a bench, with no interior and no person needed for
  MQ-02. Jinu's "second drawer" **(shipped)** is behind that door and stays there.
- **Where:** the quay-facing front room of the west house, at the shore end of the pole walk. The boy's run is
  then short and in plain sight: stall, pole walk, door, and back. A child should be able to watch it happen.
- New: the family that keeps the office lives in the rest of that house.

### 2. Miri's workshop and the ferry steps: on this map, at the east end

- **The ferry steps are a stair street** that climbs north from the shore end of the ferry jetty. PQ-01 hangs
  lanterns "across the steps", has a "bottom step" and a "top step", and has "the quay below them" **(script)**.
  The blockout has grass there now (east of the small house); it needs to become steps, two cells wide.
- **Miri's workshop is the blockout's small east house,** with its door on the top step. Crates are carried "up
  the steps and into the workshop", and Miri calls "from the workshop door" during the fight **(script)**. The
  inside (K.3, and its cutscene) is a small interior of its own, not part of this board.
- **The crates stay where they are,** on the jetty by the ferry **(shipped)**.
- **"Forty steps"** is Kaya's count, not a measure **(shipped)**. On the board the walk from the crates to the foot
  of the steps should be four to six cells and fit in one view, so the table can see how short it is and watch
  her not take it. In K.2 the ladder is "tied to a level pole" **(script)**: give the foot of the steps a painted
  pole of its own at the quay edge.
- **The street itself runs off the top edge** from the head of the steps. It is the street where Kaya was born
  **(script; owner, 2026-11-17)**. The chandler's shutter is "two doors along" and closes in narration (K.5), so
  it may be off the board; if there is room for one more shop front in sight of the top step, it is his.
- **The collectors' fight (K.4)** is "the ferry steps and the quay below them" **(script)**. Treat it as its own
  fight board when PQ-01 is built, as the pontoon is. The explore map does not need to be fight-sized there.
- Hanru's letter in Scene 3.7 waits "at the ferry steps" **(script)**: the same place.

### 3. The outreach room: its door is on this map; the room is a small interior

- **Which story it serves:** TR-02, Aru's lesson, mornings, after MQ-03 **(script: `teachers.md`)**. PQ-03 does not
  play here. The bible opens PQ-03 at this room; the owner moved Aru's table to Causeway (2026-11-15) and the PQ-03
  script plays wholly in Causeway. The room "stays at Saltstep" **(owner)**. Build to the owner's decision.
- **What it is:** "a net store with a whitewashed wall": one bench, one basin, a shelf of jars, a kettle
  **(script)**.
- **Where:** new: a small net store on the shore lane between the west house and Jin's office, on the fishers'
  way from the pole walk. About two cells by two, door to the lane. Its whitewashed wall is the only white wall on
  the board, which is how a child finds it again after Emberwatch.
- **In MQ-02 the door is shut** and nothing is asked of it. The board only needs the building.

## The five placeholders

### 1. The bell

- **Where it hangs:** on **the level pole**, the tallest of the painted poles, where the pole walk meets the
  platforms. This is the pole the clerk reads the packet out at **(shipped: "at the level pole at noon")**, so the
  bell, the reading and the children are one spot. The brief's bell post is this pole; its painted board shows the
  pattern in painted marks, with no words.
- **It is not the ferry bell.** The ferry rings its own at the landing **(shipped)**.
- New: **what it rings.** One stroke at each turn of the tide, when stallholders check their mooring ropes. The
  emergency pattern is three quick strokes and a gap, over and over, and it means one thing: leave the planks and
  go up the steps.
- New: **the adults' argument** **(bible: "several adults insist on arguing about it")** is whether the gap comes
  after three strokes or four. The children have it right, because they are the ones who practise.
- New: **the children's game.** One child claps a pattern. If it is the real one, everybody runs to the shore end
  of the pole walk and touches the first pole; if it is a false one, whoever moved is out. The rope is tied off
  above a child's reach, so they clap it and never ring it. The worn ring on the planks is from this game.

### 2. The fishers (the racks, the boats)

- They come into the basin on the rising tide and sell from the boat. New: the basin shallows as the water drops,
  so a boat that argues too long has to leave or sit on the mud. That is why the bargainers keep moving
  **(bible)**, and why fish gets cheaper as the tide goes out.
- They resent company timetables that ignore the weather, and so do the pilots **(bible)**.
- Fish goes boat, rack, stall **(brief)**; nets go up the pole walk to the net store. The fish-seller empties her
  bucket off the right-hand side of the spare platform **(script)**. One fisher has a story about a fish that owes
  him money (SQ-07) **(bible)**: leave a dock winch near the racks for it.

### 3. The dock workers (the freight side)

- They are paid by the crate, and a short load comes out of their own pay **(script: PQ-01's foreman)**. They load
  for whoever is hiring, which this season is mostly the contractor's barges **(shipped)**.
- Half of them have carried a crate they did not like the weight of **(shipped: Tavi)**. They talk about weight,
  not about what is inside.
- Fish porters carry on yokes, and a child's yoke is a smaller one **(script, TR-03)**. A porter called Bem has a
  bad back from a crate marked "light" **(script, TR-02)**; he is not needed on the board before then.

### 4. The children (under the bell)

- They know the pattern and play the game above. They also run the quay's errands: the boy the clerk sends is one
  of them **(shipped)**.
- They have opinions and give them. In PQ-01 "the children of the pole walk" decide which lantern is best
  **(script)**.
- They watch strangers work to see if they know what they are doing **(script: the girl with two buckets)**.

### 5. The washing gap: who lives there

New: the gap runs between the west house and the net store. The house belongs to the family that keeps the courier
office, and they have taken in a newly arrived family in the back room **(bible: "recently arrived families share
the shore")**. So there are two households' washing on one line: one end faded and patched, the other end with
more children's clothes than the house has ever had. Nobody is named. The gap is a short cut because the children
of both families use it.

### 6. The gulls' post: something about the tide

- New: the waste end is the oldest piece of the market, a fixed stage on piles from before the platforms floated.
  It is the first thing under at high water, which is why the waste goes there: the tide carries it off twice a
  day.
- New: the post is an old level pole whose paint has worn off. What it has is a weed line, and the weed line is
  higher than the top painted band on the new poles. An attentive player learns two things: read the bands to
  know the water, and the water has been higher than the town has paint for.
- The gulls leave the post when the tide turns, a little before the bell.

### 7. What is sold besides broth

- **From the boats:** river fish and bay fish over the side **(shipped: river fish)**; new: crabs in wet baskets
  tied shut with reed, and shellfish by the scoop.
- **From the platforms:** new: dried and smoked fish off the racks, salt in paper twists, pickles in jars
  **(script: every household has jars to lend)**, plum buns **(script: Oko's)**, rope, hooks and cork floats.
- **From the shore end:** cabbages and tea come down the coast road by cart **(shipped: Bo-shan's cabbages)**.
  Lanterns are six a string, in season, at the ferry steps **(script)**.
- **One man sells one spoon** **(shipped)**. Leave him his plank on the pole walk.
- At the lowest water, temporary stalls go up on the exposed stages and the bargaining gets silly **(bible: the
  Low-Tide Market)**. That is a later event; the board only needs the waste end and one bare stage.

### Behind the quay

New: the stone houses nearest the water belong to old fishing and pilot families, and a house passes down with
its mooring. Dock porters and ferry hands rent rooms further up. The families who arrived lately live at the low
end, where forty households carry their water **(shipped: Veyra)** and where the Emberwatch clinic's supplies go
**(script, 3.1)**.

## Where the documents disagree

- **The courier office.** The issue reads Scene 2.1 as sending the party there. The built text sends a boy. Built
  text wins; nothing to change.
- **PQ-03's place.** The bible says the Saltstep outreach room; the owner's decision and the script say Causeway.
  The room is kept for TR-02.
- **PQ-01's timing.** Act III's hero table says it "runs at Saltstep" in Act III; the PQ-01 script opens it on the
  ferry's return after MQ-03 and leaves it open. The board should be ready for it from the first return.
- **"The low landing".** Always Causeway's. Saltstep has "the low end".
- **"The ferry steps".** Luli's line (3.1) and Hanru's letter (3.7) use the name before PQ-01 shows the place. All
  three mean the stair street at the Saltstep landing.
