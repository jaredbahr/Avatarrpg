has gone. The driver (Bo-shan, or Dorin on the Act I loss branch) is with the party, on foot. Somebody has
taken them in (`sheltered_by`: the Six-Ladle Kitchen, a rail crew's bunk wagon, Tavi's ferry, or Hua's house).

separate Dorin line is given (the narrator's "Bo-shan" becomes "Dorin" too). In MQ-11 Bo-shan is freed and

Themes: equality, responsibility, belonging. The people accused of sabotage are hidden by a cook, or a rail
crew, or a ferry pilot, or a stranger. An assembly's platform has only stairs until the floor floods; then
there is a ramp, and nobody remarks on it. A mother hands her daughter a slip of paper.
