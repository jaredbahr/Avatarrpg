and enlarged the guide and introduced a bottom notch; do not pack it. v2 restores
margin/shape substantially but is not exact registration. Both selected sources
still show saturated edge fringe. Measure source corners and internal material
boundaries against actual projected cells before runtime use. Source inspection
does not prove accurate collision, cover, water, depth, scale or default play zoom.

the Cutting's road and grass and abandons authored composition. Packing should
preserve the reviewed full-scene painting, correct measured registration, clip
to exact authoritative boundaries and split into the established scene pages.
