## Who

A man in his fifties with grey-streaked hair cropped close, a scar through one eyebrow, a lined face that is tired and proud in equal measure. Weathered olive skin.

A worn provincial guard captain’s coat in faded grey-brown (base #8d7d69, shadow #4a3a2c) with the insignia torn off the breast, leaving a darker patch; a sabre hilt at the hip.

One hand resting on the sabre hilt, not gripping it.

## Signature

The scar, the cropped grey hair and the torn insignia patch. He looks like a captain who has stopped being one.
