# Illustrated story breaks — The Common Current

Reviewed against the 17 September 2026 Story Bible v1.0, its main campaign and
reveal ladder, and A World Worth Wandering companion draft 1.0. The current
repository implements the quarry adventure only. This document separates the
scenes wired into that adventure from placements for later missions.

No new cutscene is inserted inside Ruon's surrender or custody choice. Let his
words and the players' decision carry that scene. Mira and Pella remain people
the player talks to, not mandatory movies that duplicate those conversations.
Endings keep the original complete summary for review and saving.

Captions are the existing story text, not a second script that can contradict
it. Play is optional and duration follows reading length, not a fixed 60-second
quota. A two-line travel beat stays short. The final caption waits for Continue.
All frames are stationary; a held painting can span several captions when a
new angle adds nothing. New scenes should earn their interruption.

## Optional world moments

These are player-invited observations, not forced interruptions on every visit.

- **Dorin and Deputy (SQ-31 / DLG-13):** in safe Ba Dan, show Dorin's board,
  the turtle duck beside it, then Dorin making room for its chin. Preserve the
  companion's short exchange: Pella calls it Deputy; Dorin calls it a turtle
  duck; she says it can be two things; he accidentally addresses it as Deputy.
  Return to the offered conversation or road. Nothing implies the rescue is over.
- **The Bent Ladle (DLG-14):** two views from Pemi's boat show why the best table
  changes with the berth. Keep her menu joke optional for a selected Tenzo.
  Return to a meal, the view or departure; no hidden emergency behind hospitality.
- **The meadow (SQ-33):** Brindle resting, six relaxed feet, picnic at a sensible
  distance. Viri sets expectations; Sena and Nima appear only when present.
  The party's kindness does not force the animal to stand or earn ownership.
- **The cutting (SQ-32):** tracks, distant badgermole, newly shifted earth. Hold
  distance and scale. No taming montage, bending lesson or secret plot clue.
- **The distant wake (SQ-35):** optional after safe post-campaign travel, under
  every ending. Horizon; immense living contour; water falling; an empty sea.
  Let the pictures hold for 30–45 seconds with little text. No prophecy, new
  power, mandatory boarding or repeatable sightseeing promise.

## Authoring constraints

Use scene-specific gates when these missions are implemented; the mission IDs
above are Bible identifiers, not existing engine node IDs. Never mark future
content as available merely because an art frame exists. Leisure stops during
the final commitment interval and returns when travel is safe.

Show named heroes only when selected and actually present. Prefer scenery and
local people for shared establishing shots. Ruon's trial/trade, every defeat,
Bo-shan's actual rescue and individual accountability remain separate facts.
No protagonist delivers the player's choice in an unskippable movie. No new
reveal arrives earlier than the Bible's ladder. Repeated visits should usually
return control immediately, with replay offered rather than forced.
