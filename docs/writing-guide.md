# Writing Ba Dan

Use this alongside `CLAUDE.md` when adding story content. The cast is original;
the Avatar franchise supplies the setting and broad creative inspiration.

## What to carry forward

Make bending part of ordinary work: cooking, surveying, fishing, hauling stone.
Let travel bring people with different customs into contact. Give non-benders
useful skills, commitments and opinions of their own. Industrial machinery should
affect the people who work around it and the water they drink.

Keep room for animal trouble, awkward hospitality and jokes between people who
know each other. A serious scene can be funny without making someone's distress
the punchline. Let kindness cost a little effort: washing a cup, waiting for a
straggler, feeding another person. The action can carry the meaning.

| Sen     | The absent crews and keeping their rest stop open  | Offers company by finding something practical to do             |

These are starting points, not verbal tics to repeat in every appearance. The
playable characters' bios provide further habits and motives. Read dialogue aloud;
use contractions where the speaker would use them.

## Revision checks

- Ask what the speaker wants from this conversation. Cut lines that exist only
  to announce the theme or explain the writer's intention.
- Replace vague menace and automatic cliffhangers with something the player can
  observe. Leave uncertainty where a character genuinely lacks information.
- Watch for repeated sentence fragments, rhetorical reversals, three-part
  slogans and a clever final sentence on every exchange. Vary the rhythm.
- Keep a proverb when someone has a reason to say or write it. Do not make every
  villager deliver one. The riverside shrine can have an inscription without the
  narrator interpreting it afterwards.
- State the stakes of choices clearly. NPCs may judge a decision; the choice UI
  should explain its consequences without declaring the correct moral answer.
- Check every route: victory, defeat, negotiation, trade, optional visits and
  repeat visits. Do not assume a particular party size or an optional conversation.
- Keep journal entries within what the player has already learned. A first-visit
  flag cannot establish something that happens only on a later visit.
- Check tactical claims against the actual content and rules. Humour must not
  promise a reward, status effect or shop mechanic the game does not implement.

Preserve node IDs, links, conditions, flags, rewards and character kits during a
prose pass. Illustrated scenes have one shot per line; keep those counts aligned.
Run `npm run verify` before pushing and let the required browser and screenshot
checks run on the current PR revision.
