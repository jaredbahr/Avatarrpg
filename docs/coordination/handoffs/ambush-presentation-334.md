- **Behavior drafted:** `App` holds the final drawn explore-trail seats only for a walked battle entry, outside `GameState` and saves. During the fresh combat opening, `CombatScene` plays each party member from that seat to the existing battle spawn through the normal 750 ms-per-tile stroll cadence. The hold waits for those tracks; reduced motion skips them. A pure core return search preserves an already clear crossing; otherwise it chooses the nearest reachable dry road seat clear of props and painted rubble (walkable ground only if no road is reachable). The reducer applies it on matching walked battle triggers for victory and defeat, updating the immediate dialogue location and remembered explore return.
