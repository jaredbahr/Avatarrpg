Not shipped. Made by `FNT_DEMO_PRACTICE_ONLY=1 FNT_DEMO_LABEL=<label> npx playwright test -c playwright.ba-dan-demo.config.ts`
on `main` (5a41f98, `fx-before`) and on this branch (`fx-g1`), and by `playwright.deserter-fx.config.ts` (real reducer
commands: the deserter's oil flask, then a fire blast landing on Sura at the quarry gate) on both.

- `practice-<backend>-<who>-<moment>-4x.webp`: Kaya (fire), Sura (water) and Bo (earth) at gather, release and impact,
  4x nearest, **before left, after right** (`tools/crops.py`). Gather and release are cropped on the bender, impact on
  the target on the canal bank.
- `combat-<backend>-hit-<ms>ms-4x.webp`: the 96 px combat camera at 1148 ms (the blast in flight) and 1380 ms (the hit,
  damage 14), before left, after right.
- `frame-webgl-<who>-<moment>.webp`: the whole after frame.

The contact frames (release, and the burst's first cel) are drawn with the bend flash (ADR 0055), so their tones are
brightened on purpose.
