# Ba Dan market objects, PixelLab G family (issue #284, route B objects)

The figure-scale objects for the demo slice's canal market corner (`src/content/scenes/baDan.ts`, tiles x 0..13, y 0..9, plus
the north display and its baskets just east of it at x 14), generated by the PixelLab generator Kaya's G sheet came from
(Pro Flash, high top-down). These are **source candidates only**. Nothing here is integrated: the baked objects are still in
the Ba Dan painting, `baDan.art.ts` is untouched, and no footprint or depth key has changed. Integration (cleaning the baked
objects out of the ground with `scripts/art/ba-dan-regions/`, adding these as pieces with footprints, depth keys, contact
and key-light shadow) is the next step.

## Provenance

- Generator: PixelLab, `POST /v2/edit-image-pro-flash` (method `text`, `no_background: true`). Kaya's G character
  (`Kaya G base v2 brown boots`, idle state `e8c6638a-ac14-44d3-9373-b4f632fab7bb`) was made with `generation_mode: pro_flash`
  at the high top-down view, so this is the same model family.
- Input to every selected job: a construction blockout drawn by `tools/blockout.py` at an exact game size (below).
- Every call, its prompt, seed, input SHA-256, output SHA-256, cost and verdict: `ledger.json`. Specs to re-run a call:
  `specs/<id>.json` (`python3 -I tools/pl.py specs/<id>.json`; the key comes from `PIXELLAB_API_KEY` and is never written).
  Pro Flash records the seed but says the same seed does not promise the same image, so the raw outputs are kept.
- Generated: 2026-10-27. Terms: https://pixellab.ai/termsofservice
- Style references: `ref/kaya-idle-*.png` (Kaya's G idle rotations) and `ref/thug-*.png` (the thug G base), downloaded from
  the same account; used for judging and for the one reference-method trial.
- Generations: **171** reported by the jobs, plus one map-objects probe whose cost the API did not return. The account is
  shared with other sessions that were generating at the same time, so the balance readings in the ledger are account-wide.

Shared prompt (each object adds its own sentence, see `specs/`):

> Keep every shape, size, position and the camera angle exactly as drawn; only redraw the surfaces, wood grain, weave, cloth
> and goods in a careful hand-drawn pixel style. _[object sentence]_ High top-down view on a 2:1 isometric axis. Dark brown 1
> pixel outline, muted earth colours, 3 to 4 flat tone steps per material, warm light from the upper left, matte, no white
> highlights, no gloss, no bevelled plank edges, no shine rings on pottery, no text.

## Why blockouts

The 2026-10-13 trials failed on size and projection: the tool will not shrink or re-project on request. Probe round 1 confirmed
it (`review/probe-round1-1x.png`): Pro Flash _create_ from text alone drew the table at half the size the canvas allowed with
baskets oversized against it; map-objects with an init image kept a grey background and barely changed the input; Pro Flash
_edit_ over a measured 2:1 blockout kept size, axis and construction exactly and redrew the surface. So size, axis,
construction and arrangement are decided by measurement in `tools/blockout.py`, and the generator only renders.

## Scale

Adult **H = 89 screen px**: Kaya's idle stands 158 PixelLab px, drawn at 0.5625 screen px per PixelLab px (0.75 into the
128 px frame, 0.75 on screen at scale 1.5). A tile edge is 107.3 screen px. Each object has a grain `f`, screen px per
source px: 0.5625 (the characters' own) for everything up to one tile; **0.62 for the three 2x1 tables**, because Pro Flash
edits at most 256 px wide and a table of brief length does not fit at 0.5625. The 10% coarser source pixel is under one
screen pixel either way. `blockouts/meta.json` records each object's canvas, grain, ground anchor and footprint offset.

## Faults, against the three questions of #284

**1. Drawn by the same hand at normal play size?** Closer than any earlier trial, not yet a match.

- Contour: the objects' outlines are dark brown but lighter and softer than Kaya's, so at 1x the tables read paler and lower
  contrast than the figures. Median value 0.39–0.53 against Kaya's 0.33.
- Colour treatment differs: the edit quantises each object to 27–51 colours, while Kaya's cel carries about 3,900 (soft
  anti-aliased edges). Both are smoothed by the 0.5625 reduction, but the objects' flatter clusters are visible at 4x.
- Wood is cooler and greyer than the warm earth of the figures (the south table most).
- Near-white pixels: none in five objects; 44 in the south table (pale grain in the open sack mouths), 10 in the south baskets
  (a pale cloth the generator invented in the greens basket), 2 in the pottery. No white fruit glints, no golden plank bevels
  and no shine rings on the pottery.
- The fence has the least surface work; its rails read plain.

**2. Could these people use, carry and build these things?** Mostly yes.

- Table tops at 0.49–0.52H (hip height), baskets about a shoulder width, fruit well under half a head, bench seat at knee
  height, rack within reach. Tables have end rails and a stretcher joining the legs; sacks, baskets and jars rest on the
  planks or the ground.
- The balance on the south table is drawn small and thin; at 1x it reads as a post with a bar.
- Crate fruit in `shop-stack` is laid in tidy rows, which reads more like a display than stock.
- The pottery table's under-table jars read as floating at 1x without a contact shadow (they stand on the ground at the
  back, which the flat review ground cannot show).

**3. Does it belong to this place (contact, shadow, overlap, use)?** Not judgeable yet, and not claimed.

- No contact or key-light shadow, no grass over the feet, no ground wear: all of that is the integration step, and the
  baked objects are still in the painting.
- Use cues are present (open sacks, a scoop, nested empty baskets, stock under and beside tables, a cloth), but the goods
  are still cleaner and more evenly arranged than the brief's "evidence of use".

## Grain ladder exports (G1, G2)

Asked for by the main session on 2026-10-27 for the parallel grain-ladder study (G1: 1 art px = 1 world px, 64 x 32 tiles,
figures about 76 px; G2: 1 art px = 2 world px, 32 x 16 tiles, figures about 38 px). `tools/grain.py` writes `grain/g1/`,
`grain/g2/`, `grain/grain.json` and `review/grain-ladder-g1-g2.png` (each object beside Kaya reduced the same way: G1 at 1x
and 2x nearest, G2 at 1x and 4x nearest).

- **Assumption:** the message gave only the figure height, so G1 is set by Kaya's idle standing 76 px; each object is reduced
  by `grain_f * 76 / 89` from its source (0.480; 0.529 for the 2x1 tables). G1 is a pixel reduction (majority alpha,
  contour kept on the silhouette, otherwise each block's most frequent colour; no new colours).
- **G2** is the exact 2x nearest-neighbour downsample of G1. The cleanup is **automatic, not by hand**: silhouette edges whose
  2 x 2 block held contour become contour; single-pixel islands and holes are removed.
- **Faults:** G1 reads cleanly. G2 keeps silhouettes and outlines, but nearest neighbour picks one texture pixel per block,
  so wood and wicker become noise, fruit and basket contents turn to mush, the fence rails break into stair-step dashes,
  and Kaya's own G2 face blows out. G2 needs real hand cleanup, or a reduction that groups tones before it picks pixels; as
  exported it is not a fair test of the G2 grain.
